“Preserve what others overlooked. Follow the evidence. Keep the record open.”

Arcade Side B is an ongoing research archive devoted to the overlooked corners of arcade history— the games, companies, cabinets, ideas, and people whose stories have often been scattered, forgotten, or poorly documented.

We follow the best evidence we can find, distinguish fact from uncertainty, and consider nothing here beyond correction or improvement. New information, conflicting evidence, photographs, documents, and firsthand knowledge are always welcome.

Not everything documented here was famous, successful, or even great. It mattered because it was part of arcade history, and the people who created it deserve to have their work remembered.
